Call for emergency help if there is…

  • Chest pain or pressure, or sudden trouble breathing
  • Signs of stroke: face drooping, arm weakness, slurred speech
  • Heavy bleeding that will not stop
  • Fainting, unconsciousness or a seizure
  • Severe allergic reaction, poisoning or choking
  • Thoughts of harming yourself

While waiting for help

  • Stay calm and stay with the person.
  • Tell the operator your exact location and what happened.
  • Do not give food or drink to someone who is unconscious.
  • Unlock the door and have someone wait outside to guide the ambulance.
